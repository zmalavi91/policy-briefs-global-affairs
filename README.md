## About This Repository

This repository contains policy briefs and applied research on contemporary issues in Political Science and International Relations.

The briefs are designed for:

- Think tanks.
- Universities.
- NGOs.
- International organizations.
- Human-rights institutions.
- Policy institutes.
- Government and public-policy researchers.
- Journalists and public scholars.
- Students of International Relations and Political Science.

The purpose of each brief is to connect:

- Evidence.
- Political analysis.
- Human-rights principles.
- International law.
- Geopolitical context.
- Institutional practice.
- Practical policy recommendations.

## Policy-Brief Format

Each brief follows a consistent structure:

1. Executive summary.
2. Problem definition.
3. Background.
4. Key evidence.
5. Stakeholder analysis.
6. Policy options.
7. Risks and limitations.
8. Recommendations.
9. References.
10. Author note.

The briefs are analytical and educational. They do not represent the official position of any government, international organization, or client.
